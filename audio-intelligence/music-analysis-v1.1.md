# Clip-relative music analysis extension

The existing `version: 1` result is unchanged; the optional `temporalV1` object has its own `version: 1` and applies only to the same decoded 15-second clip. Older results and clients remain valid: the staging HTTP gateway emits the extension only when the client opts in with `X-Mumoo-Music-Features: temporal-v1`; requests without the header receive the unchanged strict v1 shape. The original file stays in the browser. The staging client sends a 16 kHz clip, so information above 8 kHz is unavailable. The existing Beat This! beat grid and tempo ambiguity, spectral frames and onsets are unchanged.

This extension reuses the existing 1,024-sample STFT at 22,050 Hz. It groups frames into 15 consecutive one-second bins, each with onset count and four ratios of squared STFT magnitude: low [20, 250), low-mid [250, 500), mid/high-mid [500, 4,000), and high [4,000, 8,000) Hz. Ratios sum to one within the specified range (allowing rounding); they are relative energy in the mixed signal, not isolated instruments. Frames are centered as in v1. Bin relative energy is mean RMS divided by the largest bin mean RMS within this clip; it is not comparable loudness across songs. For effective silence (peak bin RMS ≤ 1e-6) energy and band shares are null.

Candidate segment boundaries compare the two seconds before and after each whole-second boundary. A change of at least 0.22 in clip-relative energy is retained subject to two-second spacing and at most six segments. Segments summarize means of the constituent bins, detected onset rate per second, beat count, and change in relative energy from the preceding segment. These are conservative measured changes, not verified drops, builds, bars, emotion, stems or musical structure. Segment boundaries need not be beats. Raw unverified downbeat candidates are never used as authoritative edits.

The browser sends the private staging gateway only a bounded subset of this timeline, plus existing creator-selected genre and measured beat/onset data. It must not send raw audio or lyric text to the Brain. The selected clip hash continues to bind the analysis to the input; any original source metadata remains local.

Tests cover silence, sparse beats, a step in dynamics and existing tempo ambiguity. Real-song comparisons and performance results are research artifacts, not claims of automatically measured creative quality.
