# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** 19 November 2026  
**Contact:** moo@mumoo.app

## Free trial
Every new account receives one free Creative. This free Creative is the trial that allows you to evaluate the Service before purchasing credits.

## Credits
- Credits are sold in one-time packs.
- Credits never expire while the account exists.
- **Credits are non-refundable.**

We rely on the exception to the right of withdrawal for digital content under applicable electronic commerce rules where a free trial has been provided and the service has been commenced. This reliance must be confirmed by qualified legal counsel before the Service is offered to the public.

## Mandatory refunds
We will provide a refund where required by law, including (but not limited to):
- A double charge or clear payment error.
- A render that fails completely and cannot be corrected or re-run by us.
- Other situations where mandatory consumer protection law in your jurisdiction requires a refund.

Polar acts as Merchant of Record and handles the actual payment and any approved refund processing.

## How to request
Contact moo@mumoo.app with your account email, order details (from Polar), and a clear description of the issue. We will respond promptly.

## Chargebacks
Initiating a chargeback without first contacting us may result in account suspension while the matter is resolved.
