# Short Data Processing Agreement (Smart Link Ad Forwarding)

**Last updated:** 18 November 2026  
**Operator / Processor:** The individual operating mumoo.app  
**Contact:** moo@mumoo.app

This short Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) applies only when an artist (“Controller”) enables server-side forwarding of Smart Link visit and click events to Meta or TikTok.

## 1. Roles
- The artist is the controller of the personal data of fans that is forwarded for advertising measurement.
- mumoo acts solely as processor for this specific activity.

## 2. Processing
mumoo will forward, on the artist’s instructions, the following data for each visit or click on a Smart Link the artist has connected:
- IP address
- Browser / user-agent details
- Ad click IDs (fbclid, ttclid, gclid)
- Timestamp and related event data

Forwarding occurs only when the artist has actively connected a Meta Pixel/Conversions API token or TikTok Pixel/Events API token. Tokens are stored encrypted.

## 3. Processor obligations
mumoo will:
- Process the data only on the documented instructions of the artist (i.e., the act of connecting the token and leaving forwarding enabled).
- Ensure that persons authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality.
- Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures.
- Not engage sub-processors for this forwarding beyond the infrastructure already described in the Privacy Policy, without notice.
- Assist the artist, to the extent reasonably possible, with data-subject requests relating to the forwarded data.
- Delete or return the data at the end of the provision of the service (in practice, data is forwarded in real time and not retained by mumoo for this purpose beyond short-term operational needs).
- Make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance.

## 4. International transfers
Data may be transferred to Meta and TikTok (and their infrastructure) which may be located outside the artist’s jurisdiction. The artist is responsible for ensuring a lawful basis and any required transfer mechanisms for their own advertising use.

## 5. Term
This DPA applies for as long as the artist keeps ad forwarding enabled. It ends when the artist disconnects the token or deletes the Smart Link / account.

## 6. Liability and general
Liability is governed by the main Terms of Service. This DPA does not create additional liability beyond what is stated there.

By enabling ad forwarding the artist agrees to this DPA.
