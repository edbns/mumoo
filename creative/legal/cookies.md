# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** 18 November 2026  
**Operator:** The individual operating mumoo.app  
**Contact:** moo@mumoo.app

This Cookie Policy explains how mumoo uses cookies and similar technologies.

## Current practice
- **Smart Link (fan) pages:** No cookies and no tracking scripts are used.
- **Artist application:** Currently we do not set non-essential cookies. Essential cookies or local storage may be used for sign-in sessions and basic functionality.

## Planned: Google Analytics
We plan to add Google Analytics. When we do:

- Google Analytics will set cookies (and similar technologies) to understand how artists use the Service (page views, feature usage, approximate location, device, etc.).
- These are non-essential / analytics cookies.
- We will show a cookie consent banner before setting them.
- You will be able to accept or reject analytics cookies.
- You can change your choice later or use browser controls to block cookies.

Google Analytics data is processed by Google. See Google’s privacy information for details. We will configure it to minimise data where possible.

## Managing cookies
You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect sign-in and core features. Blocking analytics cookies will not prevent you from using the Service.

## Changes
We will update this policy when we introduce new cookies (including Google Analytics) and adjust the consent banner accordingly.
